Flatten AreaChart, BarChart, HorizontalBarChart, LineChart and ScatterChart styles. Register their styles during rendering, support provider CSS layers, and expose stable root, SVG part and animation modifier classes for consumer overrides, plus root `--variant-*` modifiers (and AreaChart `--stacked`). Rename legacy root/container and entrance classes, and replace negative-bar and zero-gridline state classes with data attributes.
