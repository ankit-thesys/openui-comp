## Limits standard optimization does not remove

Per-component delivery, removing duplicate shell definitions and lazy immutable module-local compiled records may reduce generation and payload. They do not prove the inherited private Goober parser generally correct, the hand-owned cascade guards safe for arbitrary host CSS/layer orders, strict CSP compatibility, fragment SSR/remount support, full export/state/portal coverage, package-root tree shaking, multi-version dedupe, or a production capacity/memory bound. No architecture/ownership/acceptance decision follows automatically. Preserve private patch/guard maintenance obligations and untested paths in final reporting.
